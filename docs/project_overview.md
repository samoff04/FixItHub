# FixitHub Project Overview

## Problem

Many college students want to participate in hackathons but struggle to find teammates with similar interests, complementary skills, or the same goals.

## Proposed Solution

FixitHub aims to provide a platform where students can discover and connect with potential teammates based on their skills, interests, and hackathon goals.

## Target Users

- College students
- Hackathon participants
- Students looking for project teammates
- Students interested in collaborating with like-minded peers

## Initial Goals

- Enable students to create profiles
- Help students discover potential teammates
- Make team formation easier
- Encourage collaboration beyond existing friend circles

## Future Scope

- Skill-based teammate recommendations
- Hackathon-specific matching
- Team creation and invitations
- Direct messaging
- College-based communities
